# PV Engineering Standards — Reference Summary

*This document summarizes, in original wording, the scope and purpose of
standards commonly referenced in PV system design. It does NOT reproduce
the actual text, clauses, or tables of any standard — those are licensed
documents (IEC, IEEE) or freely available primary text (NEC/NFPA) that
you should obtain directly from the issuing body and place in this
folder alongside this summary.*

## IEC 61730 — Photovoltaic (PV) Module Safety Qualification

Covers construction requirements and testing for PV module safety,
addressing electrical shock, fire, and mechanical hazards over the
module's service life. Relevant when specifying or approving panel
models for a design. Issued by the International Electrotechnical
Commission; purchase via iec.ch or a national standards body.

## IEC 62109 — Safety of Power Converters for Use in PV Power Systems

Covers safety requirements for inverters, charge controllers, and other
power-conversion equipment used in PV systems, split across general
requirements (Part 1) and inverter-specific requirements (Part 2).
Relevant when specifying inverter/controller models. Issued by IEC.

## IEEE 1547 — Standard for Interconnecting Distributed Energy Resources

Defines technical requirements for interconnecting distributed
generation (including PV) with electric power systems: voltage/frequency
ride-through, anti-islanding protection, power quality, and
communication requirements. Relevant for any grid-tied or hybrid design
and for utility interconnection applications. Issued by IEEE.

## NEC Article 690 — Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems

Part of the U.S. National Electrical Code (NFPA 70), covering circuit
requirements, disconnect and overcurrent protection, wiring methods,
and grounding specific to PV installations. Widely referenced
internationally as a best-practice baseline even outside U.S.
jurisdictions. Issued by the National Fire Protection Association.
